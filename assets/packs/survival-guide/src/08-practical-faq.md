# Practical FAQs — Burning Man 2026

## Ice (Arctica) [2026-confirmed]
- Locations: Center Camp Plaza ("Ice Main"), 3:00 & G ("Ice Cubed"), 9:00 & G ("Ice Nine"), and bulk sales at the Large Order Outpost, 6:15 & K (the only location with non-ADA vehicle parking).
- 2026 hours: pre-event Aug 27–30: 12–5 pm; event week Aug 31–Sep 6: 10 am–5 pm (Sep 6: 12–5 pm); Exodus Monday Sep 7: 10 am–12 pm.
- Payment: the Center Camp, 3:00, and 9:00 igloos take credit cards, tap/Apple Pay, and cash; the Large Order Outpost is cash-free.
- Price: not published on the pages fetched; recent years ran about $10 per bag. [UNVERIFIED for 2026]
- Ice sales are the city's only retail commerce (plus airport fees); proceeds have traditionally benefited local communities. [latter point evergreen, UNVERIFIED 2026]

## Center Camp coffee [status confirmed through 2026 guide]
- The Center Camp Café stopped selling coffee and drinks after the 2022 decommodification/sustainability decision; nothing has replaced the sales. The 2026 Survival Guide lists no beverage commerce — ice is the only thing sold in the city.
- Center Camp remains a large shaded gathering space with performances, art, and community programming ("re-centered" as a non-commercial heart of the city since 2023–24). Bring your own coffee setup or find a camp gifting it.

## Bikes [2026-confirmed]
- The four rules: Light it, Lock it, Label it (camp name + address), and Limit speed to 5 mph (including e-bikes).
- Best playa bikes: fat-tire cruisers or mountain bikes; skinny road tires fail in soft playa. Simple single-speeds survive dust better than derailleurs and cable brakes. Arrive with the bike in working order — repair camps have limited capacity.
- Lights: headlight (aimed so it doesn't blind), red tail light, plus decorative lighting so you can find it parked. Unlit riders are "darkwads."
- Never lock a bike to guy wires or structures. Secure decorations so they don't shed MOOP.
- Community bikes (bright green): free, short one-way trips only; never lock, keep at camp, decorate, or take home; report broken ones to the Yellow Bike Shop at 5:30 & D.
- Over 1,700 bikes were abandoned in 2024 — take yours home or donate it properly; abandonment violates Leave No Trace.

## Porta-potty etiquette [evergreen]
- Only human waste and single-ply toilet paper go in the potty. If it didn't come out of your body (plus single-ply TP), it doesn't go in — wipes, tampons, trash, and multi-ply paper jam the pump trucks and get banks taken out of service.
- Sit rather than hover; hovering makes messes. Close the lid, keep the door latched against wind.
- Potty banks are marked at night by solar blue lights; last outbound potties during Exodus are at the F Lots.
- Peeing on the playa is prohibited (it's public land and it's MOOP).

## Cameras, photography, photos, and consent [2026-confirmed]
- Photography rules: as a condition of entry, participants agree to ASK PERMISSION before photographing or recording anyone. Ask first — every time, everyone.
- Images from Black Rock City may not be used for any commercial, promotional, editorial, or otherwise non-personal purpose unless the photographer registered as professional media in advance and received permission from Burning Man Project.
- Permitted professional media are credentialed and their cameras carry a unique Media ID tag. If someone is being disrespectful with a camera, note their Media # and bring it to Media Mecca (6:15 & Esplanade); if they have no Media Tag, find a Black Rock Ranger immediately.
- Drones/UAVs must be pre-registered; if you are not sure you have permission to bring a drone, you don't — leave it at home.
- If your picture or your images turn up used without permission: press@burningman.org.

## Dogs and other animals [2026-confirmed]
- Dogs and other animals — including emotional-support animals — are NOT permitted at the event. Show up with an animal and you will not be admitted. The only exception is a service animal trained to perform a task for a person's disability.
- Black Rock City is loud, hot, and frightening for animals; the rule protects them as much as people.

## Hot springs and packages [2026-confirmed]
- Local hot springs are OFF LIMITS during the event under the BLM permit (some are lethally hot); the BLM patrols them.
- Do not ship packages to Burning Man's Gerlach office or PO Box — they are returned. USPS General Delivery, Gerlach, NV 89412 works for a package to yourself; nothing can be delivered to you on playa.

## Other practical points [2026-confirmed unless noted]
- Playa Info (Esplanade & 5:45): open 9 am–6 pm daily; directory computers 24/7; ASL interpreters 9 am–10 pm; arranges emergency tow and lockout help. No internet access for participants.
- Airport (88NV): arriving 8 am–6 pm; Exodus 6 am–6:30 pm; valid BRC ticket plus $100 per-person airport fee; private pilots must pre-register at airport.burningman.org.
- Driving in the city: private cars may not drive during the event (park at camp); licensed mutant vehicles and registered ADA vehicles only; citywide limit is 5 mph for bikes and vehicles. [mutant-vehicle/DMV framework evergreen; 5 mph bikes 2026-confirmed]
- Cell service is unreliable to nonexistent; assume offline. [evergreen]
