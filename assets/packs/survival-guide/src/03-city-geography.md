# Black Rock City Geography and Addressing

## The clock-face grid [2026-confirmed for locations; layout evergreen]
- Black Rock City is laid out as a giant clock face: a semicircle of streets wrapped around the Man, which stands at the center.
- Radial streets are named for clock times (2:00 through 10:00, e.g. 3:00, 4:30, 7:30). The 6:00 axis runs from Center Camp straight to the Man; 12:00 points toward the Temple and deep playa. [clock range/axis orientation: evergreen]
- Annular (ring) streets are lettered outward from the center: the Esplanade is the innermost ring facing the open playa, then A, B, C ... through K.
- An address like "7:30 & G" means the intersection of the 7:30 radial street and the G ring street. To find it: follow any ring street to the 7:30 radial, or ride a radial out/in to the G ring. Lower letters are closer to the Esplanade/open playa; times near 6:00 are closer to Center Camp.
- Ring-street names change yearly with the art theme, but the letters A–K stay the addressing system. [evergreen]

## Key locations (2026 city plan) [2026-confirmed]
- The Man: center of the clock face, out on the open playa.
- The Temple: on the open playa beyond the Man along the 12:00 axis. [axis placement evergreen; porta-potty banks flank the Man along 3:00 and 9:00 per the 2026 plan]
- Center Camp Plaza: 6:15 & B (heart of the city's 6:00 neighborhood).
- Plazas: at 3:00, 4:30, 6:00, 7:30, and 9:00 on both B and G streets.
- Playa Info / Lost & Found: Esplanade & 5:45. Placement Headquarters: Esplanade & 5:45 area.
- Ranger HQ: Esplanade & 6:30; Ranger outposts at the 3:00 and 9:00 plazas.
- Medical: 3:00 & C, 9:00 & C, and the main Emergency Services (ESD) / Rampart station at 5:15 & Esplanade. Ranger HQ is the one at 6:30 & Esplanade — different address.
- Arctica ice: Center Camp Plaza, 3:00 & G, 9:00 & G, bulk at 6:15 & K.
- Yellow Bike Shop (community bikes): 5:30 & D. Recycle Camp (aluminum cans): 5:35 & Esplanade.
- Burn Gardens: Esplanade & 5:30, in front of MOOP Map HQ.
- Airport (88NV): follow the 5:00 direction out toward the perimeter fence. Box Offices at the Main Gate and the Airport.

## Camping zones [2026-confirmed]
- Main camping: the semicircle between Esplanade and K.
- Open camping (first-come): between H and K streets.
- Walk-in camping: past K Street between 2:00 and 5:00 — no vehicles.
- Placed theme camps occupy most frontage on the Esplanade, plazas, and inner streets. [evergreen]

## Open playa and deep playa [evergreen; buffer 2026-confirmed]
- The open playa (inside the Esplanade arc) is reserved for interactive art — no camping.
- "Deep playa" is the far open area beyond the Temple toward the trash fence, sparser and darker; large art and few landmarks.
- The whole city sits inside a pentagon-shaped perimeter "trash fence"; crossing it is prohibited. The Bureau of Land Management maintains a no-camping buffer zone outside the fence.
- Porta-potty banks throughout the city and on the open playa are marked at night by solar-powered blue lights.

## Navigation tips [evergreen]
- At night, orient by the Man and the lit Esplanade arc; the Temple marks 12:00.
- Ask anyone — giving directions by clock-and-letter is universal ("we're at 7:30 & G, toward the 7:30 plaza").
- The city holds roughly 80,000 people at peak.
