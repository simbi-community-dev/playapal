# MOOP, Greywater, Burn Scars, and Fire Safety

## MOOP basics [2026-confirmed]
- MOOP = Matter Out of Place: any material left on the desert surface that didn't come from the playa — from couches down to glitter, sequins, wood chips, and hair.
- Black Rock City is the largest Leave No Trace event in the world; there are no trash cans — everyone packs out everything they bring in.
- Carry a MOOP bag everywhere; do a daily MOOP sweep of your camp with a handled jug; secure loose items and costume pieces before they blow away.
- Line sweeps: camps walk shoulder-to-shoulder across their footprint picking every fragment — do one before leaving. [evergreen practice]
- The MOOP Map: after the event, the Playa Restoration team surveys every square meter of the city footprint and publishes a color-coded map (green = clean, yellow/red = problem areas) scoring each camp's location. Red scores can cost a camp its placement the following year. MOOP Map HQ is at Esplanade & 5:30. [map mechanics evergreen; HQ location 2026-confirmed]
- The event's federal permit depends on passing BLM's post-event site inspection. [evergreen]

## Greywater and blackwater [2026-confirmed]
- No greywater (wash water) may ever touch the playa — not even "just soapy water." Even treated greywater must be properly disposed of.
- Disposal options: small-footprint evaporation ponds, hauling to off-site RV dump stations, or contracting United Site Services (USS) for on-site pumping (pumping may arrive a day later than scheduled — plan capacity accordingly).
- Spill response: (1) stop the source — no more showers/dishes/toilets until fixed; (2) collect leaked fluid with buckets and dig up contaminated playa if it fits in a 5-gallon bucket; (3) repair or get pumped (flag USS trucks marked "RV"); (4) report any spill over 25 gallons to Rangers or the Environmental Compliance/ESD team immediately — blackwater or fuel spills over 25 gallons are environmental emergencies.
- Vehicle leaks: check your undercarriage daily (old RVs especially); use drip pans, tarps, or secured plywood under drips; excavate and pack out contaminated playa, then refill the hole. Leaky vehicles should be fixed before arriving.

## Fuel safety [2026-confirmed]
- Camp fuel storage cap: no more than 110 gallons or two 55-gallon drums per camp.
- Fill containers to at most 80% (heat expansion). Never set fuel containers directly on the playa, even briefly.
- All fuel containers need secondary containment holding 110% of the largest container's volume, in place before fuel is unpacked.
- Questions: fuelsafety@burningman.org.

## Burn scars and burning responsibly [2026-confirmed]
- Never burn directly on the playa: it bakes the surface into a dark, brick-like scar that lasts years and creates hazardous bumps. Volunteers have spent thousands of hours cleaning historical burn scars.
- Burn barrels: elevated at least 6 inches off the playa, contained; unpainted wood only; small amounts of paper/cardboard as kindling only with a wire-mesh ember cover. Violations risk BLM confiscation for the rest of the event.
- Never burn trash, treated/painted wood, or anything that isn't yours to burn.
- Bring a shovel and metal containers to remove your ashes; ash disposal is your responsibility.
- Community Burn Gardens (public burn platforms): Esplanade & 5:30, open Sunday Sept 6 (10 am) through Tuesday Sept 8; wood donations accepted; no cardboard, paper, compost, recycling, trash, or toxic materials.
- Art burns and the big burns happen on protected burn platforms with Fire Art Safety Team (FAST) oversight and fire perimeters — respect the perimeter until it drops. [evergreen]

## Packing out and heading home [2026-confirmed]
- Precycle before arriving: strip packaging at home, favor aluminum cans, avoid glass and styrofoam.
- Recycle Camp at 5:35 & Esplanade takes aluminum cans only (9 am–5 pm); everything else leaves with you.
- Secure your load for Exodus — highway-side MOOP from unsecured loads is a major issue; use the F Lot stop to re-check straps.
- Use legitimate trash/recycling/RV-dump locations in Nevada (the guide lists them); do not dump in Gerlach, Empire, or roadside bins.
