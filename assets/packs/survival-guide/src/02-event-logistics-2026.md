# Event Logistics — Burning Man 2026

## Dates and theme [2026-confirmed]
- Burning Man 2026 runs Sunday, August 30 through Monday, September 7, 2026 (Labor Day), in the Black Rock Desert near Gerlach, Nevada.
- 2026 theme: "Axis Mundi" — celebrates interconnectedness, shared social realities, and ties to the natural world.
- The event officially ends Monday, September 7 at 6:00 pm; Exodus (departure) support continues through 11:59 pm Tuesday, September 8.
- Man burn: Saturday night, September 5; Temple burn: Sunday night, September 6. [2026 dates per multiple 2026 guides and the standard Saturday-Man/Sunday-Temple schedule; not stated on the BMorg pages fetched — treat exact burn times as unpublished]

## Gate [2026-confirmed unless noted]
- The Gate opens to all participants at 12:01 am on Sunday, August 30, 2026, and stays open 24 hours a day during the event.
- The Gate never "closes" for departures — there is no departure gate closure: the Gate is open around the clock, and you may leave whenever you like. You do NOT have to be off the playa before the event ends (Monday, September 7 at 6:00 pm) — Exodus support continues through 11:59 pm Tuesday, September 8. Only two things ever pause the Gate: bad weather (temporary) and a lost-child alert (outbound traffic held until the child is found, usually minutes).
- Every person needs a valid ticket; every vehicle except motorcycles needs a pre-purchased Vehicle Pass.
- Vehicle Passes cost $165 each plus taxes/fees, one per purchase in current sales. [2026-confirmed]
- 2026 Main Sale ticket tiers: $550, $675, $775, $975, $1,500, $3,000 ($775 approximates the per-participant cost). OMG (last-chance) Sale opened at noon July 29, 2026, registration from July 20. A final ticket sale was open as of August 2026 with no registration required.
- At the Gate, vehicles are searched and tickets are torn/scanned. Will Call/Box Office is reachable from any lane; "Apex" kiosks about 1/4 mile before the Gate direct Will Call traffic. Bring the whole vehicle to Will Call, not just one person.
- Box Office closes at 12:00 noon on September 7, 2026 — pick up tickets/credentials before then.
- Gate Road speed limit: 10 mph. Stay in your vehicle in line; no open containers of alcohol; do not walk in traffic lanes; keep headlights on at night if stopped on Highways 447 or 34.
- Wait times in the first 36 hours after opening can be 5+ hours. Shortest lines are typically 3–10 am on non-opening days; arriving Tuesday or later avoids the rush. The Gate can close temporarily in bad weather.
- Traffic info: BMIR 94.5 FM hourly traffic reports from opening Sunday; Gate Advisory Radio Station (GARS) 95.1 FM for entry-process updates; @BManTraffic on X/Twitter; BRC Dashboard at brcdashboard.burningman.org.

## Greeters [evergreen]
- The Greeters are the volunteer crew stationed just past the Gate on the way into the city — Black Rock City's official welcome. Every arriving vehicle rolls through the Greeters station after tickets are checked.
- What they do: welcome arrivals home ("Welcome home!" is the traditional greeting), orient newcomers to the city, hand out the WhatWhereWhen event booklet and city map, and answer arrival questions — where your camp's address is, how to find Playa Info or Center Camp.
- First-timers (playa "virgins") are traditionally invited to ring the big bell and make a dust angel on the playa.
- The Greeters station is also the city's arrival-side landmark: hitchhikers wait there, and Exodus directions reference it.
- Greeters are not the Gate: the Gate crew checks tickets and searches vehicles; the Greeters welcome and orient — no ticket business at Greeters.

## Early arrival [2026-confirmed]
- Arriving before Gate opening requires a pre-approved early arrival pass (Setup Access Pass) for each person; without one, the entire vehicle is turned away and sent back toward Reno.
- Do not wait in Gerlach or along Highways 447/34 before the Gate opens. Early-window arrivals are directed down Gate Road to an opening-night staging lot.
- Burner Express bus passengers arriving before Sunday's opening do not need a Setup Access Pass; Burner Express Air passengers arriving the Saturday before opening also do not need one.

## Burner Express [2026-confirmed]
- Burner Express Bus runs from San Francisco and Reno to Black Rock City; faster entry and exit than private vehicles; optional reserved camping near the on-playa bus depot; riders may pre-buy up to 20 gallons of water per person, collected at the Bus Depot on arrival. Tickets go on sale mid-May, are non-transferable and non-refundable, and require a BRC ticket to ride. Schedule: burnerexpress.burningman.org.
- Burner Express Air flies from Reno, the Bay Area, and Southern California into BRC's airport; same 20-gallon water option (collected at the airport); tickets on sale early May and typically sell out fast; operates from a separate area of Reno-Tahoe International.
- Carpool matching: official board at spark.burningman.org; Reno Airport Rideshare via Air Playa Info near baggage claim.

## Exodus and the Pulse [2026-confirmed]
- Route out: take K Street toward 6:00, then follow signs onto Gate Road; all lanes are outbound during Exodus.
- Pulse procedure: vehicles stop and shut engines off; every hour, all vehicles advance one mile simultaneously. Pulsing does not change total departure time; it provides rest breaks and reduces idling.
- Peak waits: 6–9 hours from Greeters to the County Road 34 pavement, plus 2–3+ hours to Reno; allow up to 12 hours to reach Reno airport at peak. Most departures happen Sunday and Monday.
- Best departure windows: very early morning, late evening, or Tuesday before noon; avoid roughly 10 am–4 pm.
- Do not create alternate exits — leaving the route violates BLM rules, earns tickets, and sends you back to the end of the queue.
- F Lot ("Final Frontier") near the pavement is a reunification station and the last chance to secure your load; last porta-potties are at the F Lots.
- Lockout help is available via Playa Info; a tow service operates Saturday through Tuesday post-event (request at Playa Info). AAA and similar clubs will not enter the event site.
- Exodus radio: GARS 95.1 FM travel times several times hourly; BMIR 94.5 FM Exodus info.
- Tips: check load security (and your neighbors'), blow dust out of your engine air filter to prevent overheating, keep a spare key accessible, use the far lanes if your vehicle is struggling so tow crews can reach you. Hitchhikers should wait at Greeters.
