# The Man, the Temple, and how Burning Man started

## The Man [evergreen; 2026 burn night confirmed]
- The Man is the wooden effigy at the center of the clock face — the point every radial street aims at. The city is laid out around it.
- The Man burns Saturday night (September 5, 2026), after dark, with the Fire Conclave performing before the burn and a fire perimeter held until it drops. In recent years the burn has started around 9 pm; the exact 2026 time was not published on the pages checked. Big crowds, dust, and art cars — bring lights and agree on a meet-up spot with your camp beforehand.

## The Temple [2026-confirmed — Survival Guide "The Temple"]
- The Temple is a space for honoring, celebrating, grieving, and finding solace — memorials, quiet reflection, and sometimes weddings. It belongs to everyone; Temple Guardians keep it safe and welcoming from the moment it opens.
- Offerings: heartfelt tributes — photos, posters, messages, objects. Place them on any available surface inside, on the exterior walls, or on the surrounding fence, up to 10 feet high, without covering or climbing over another's tribute. Bring your own stapler or sturdy tape; the wind takes anything loose.
- Privacy and consent are the rule inside: photographing or recording other people or their offerings only with explicit prior consent, and that includes phones and smart glasses. Guardians remove threats, hate speech, desecration of others' offerings, weapons or intimidating objects, and safety hazards.
- The Temple burns Sunday night (September 6, 2026), after dark, in silence — no cheering, no fireworks; the whole city watches quietly. In recent years the Temple burn has started around 8 pm; exact 2026 time not published on the pages checked.
- The Temple sits on the open playa beyond the Man along the 12:00 axis. The first Temple was built in 2000 (David Best's Temple of the Mind); one has been built and burned every year since.

## History: how Burning Man started [evergreen — burningman.org Timeline + registry-verified]
- 1986: Larry Harvey and his friend Jerry James start Burning Man — they build an
  improvised wooden figure and burn it on Baker Beach in San Francisco on the
  Summer Solstice; a curious crowd gathers. That is the first Man. Burning Man
  started in 1986; it was founded by Larry Harvey and Jerry James; it started on
  Baker Beach, San Francisco, and moved to the desert in 1990. The founders of
  Burning Man are Larry Harvey and Jerry James. The year Burning Man started:
  1986. What year did it start? 1986, on Baker Beach.
- 1990: the last Baker Beach gathering — police stop the burn — and the event moves, with the Cacophony Society, to the Black Rock Desert in Nevada over Labor Day weekend. Black Rock City has risen on the Black Rock playa every year since, except 1997 (held nearby at Hualapai Playa / Fly Ranch) and 2020–2021 (cancelled for Covid-19). It returned in 2022.
- The 10 Principles were written by Larry Harvey in 2004 to describe the culture that had grown up (see the Ten Principles file). Today the city holds roughly 80,000 people at peak, and the two-lane highway in is the practical limit on its size.
- The event is put on by Burning Man Project, a nonprofit; the theme changes yearly (2026: "Axis Mundi").
