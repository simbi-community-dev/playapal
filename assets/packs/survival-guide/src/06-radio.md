# Radio in Black Rock City

## Official stations [2026-confirmed]
- BMIR 94.5 FM — Burning Man Information Radio. Broadcasts travel, emergency, and general information plus community programming 24 hours a day. Hourly Gate traffic reports start opening Sunday; Exodus-specific info at event end.
- GARS 95.1 FM — Gate Advisory Radio Station. Gate Road travel times and traffic reports several times an hour, plus entry-process updates.
- BRC 911 Dispatch (emergencies only, two-way): 451.9000 MHz with CTCSS/PL tone 91.5 programmed into a personal radio reaches Black Rock City's 911 dispatch.

## Camp-band radio culture [evergreen — community practice from ePlaya and radio forums]
- Many camps carry FRS/GMRS handhelds ("family radios") for coordination. FRS range on playa is limited (roughly half a mile); GMRS at higher power reaches from camp to deep playa; MURS (2 W) is quiet on playa and can reach much farther with a vehicle antenna.
- Etiquette: listen before transmitting; open with the name of the person/camp you're calling so others know the traffic isn't for them; keep transmissions short — channels are shared citywide.
- CTCSS "privacy" codes filter incoming chatter so a camp mostly hears its own crew (they do not make the channel private — others can still hear you).
- GMRS legally requires an FCC license; adding external antennas to FRS radios is illegal.
- The Emergency Services Department does not coordinate FRS/GMRS bands, but high-power radio setups should be reported to ESD in advance so signals can be isolated if interference occurs.
- Practical tips: agree on a camp channel+code before arrival, write it on the camp board, keep radios in ziplocks against dust, bring 12 V/solar charging. [evergreen]
