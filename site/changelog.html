The road to the playa

Playa Pal was built in one seven-day sprint before Burning Man 2026. Each tenth of a version is an era — a thing the app couldn't do the day before. 1.0 is earned when Black Rock City itself has field-tested it.

0.5.0 — The social city

Aug 19 · the first public release — the candidate that rides to the burn

  • Friends on playa — share a friend card as a QR any normal camera scans; the card travels inside the link itself: no server, no address book, no account. "Beam friends" gossips a whole crew's map; a printable paper list honors the BurnerMap tradition.
  • The offline city map — Black Rock City drawn from measured geometry: streets, plazas, toilet banks, your pins, your people, your blue dot. No tiles, no network.
  • One coherent app: three tabs, the Angel as a full-screen conversation, the real 2026 city geometry live, daily-event honesty, curation with restore, and the disclosure that the whole app is AI-written under human direction.

0.4.0 — The trained Angel and the field kit

Aug 17–18

  • The whiteout compass: offline GPS to a city address, one giant arrow with clock-phrase directions, dropped pins, "Take me home", nearest toilets, walk times on every event card.
  • An open model catalog with a device-aware chooser; retrieval ranking and disciplined thinking budgets; an eval instrument carrying its owner's scoring bar. iOS cracked — it runs on a 4 GB iPhone mini.

0.3.0 — Facts over vibes

Aug 15–16

  • A fact graph under the packs — provenance-carrying edges, a query engine, the roots of the lineage view.
  • Engine hardening, and the training program stood up: three audits over every fact before any fine-tune touched the model.
